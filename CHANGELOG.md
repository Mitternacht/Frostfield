# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- Initial Frostfield architecture and development plan.
- Ordered implementation tasks, acceptance criteria, and validation protocol.
- Claude implementation handoff and contribution guidance.
- A Swift package foundation with iOS, macOS, and visionOS 27.0 deployment
  targets and a CI build workflow. [Build evidence](docs/evidence/build-baseline.md)
- FrostfieldLab, a native example on macOS, iOS, and visionOS that renders the
  package's bundled Metal triangle and displays device or shader setup errors.
- Per-window drawable sizing and pause and resume behavior. The lab draws on
  demand by default and keeps one submitted frame in flight per window.
- An indexed cube, perspective camera, transforms, and unlit, Lambert, and
  caller-supplied Metal materials.
- A bounded GPU particle fountain, a warm color grade, and leased offscreen
  textures with producer and consumer completion tracking.
- The optional `FrostfieldSwiftUI` product and a lab fixture with a native
  rotate button.
- Nested scene nodes, shared-depth rendering, and camera picking with a
  selectable three-cube lab fixture.
- Image textures, OBJ and glTF triangle geometry, and scene transform
  keyframes with textured and animated lab fixtures.
- Static glTF node and opaque base-color material import, procedural planes
  and UV spheres, and directional shadows for Lambert and PBR materials.
- Drawable callbacks for native window hosts and SwiftUI scene graph
  presentation.
- An opt-in visionOS immersive fixture using caller-provided projections and
  reversed depth.
- glTF node animation and skin import, reusable prepared scenes, and GPU
  skeletal deformation for the color and shadow passes.
- PBR texture channels, samplers, cutouts, and premultiplied transparency.
- Typed Metal material graphs, surface flow particles, bloom, chromatic
  aberration, and film grain.
- A native Cyber Ocean reference scene with orbit, zoom, playback, and Mac
  offscreen capture.
- HDR environment preprocessing, environment backgrounds, contact-hardening
  directional shadows, thin-lens depth of field, and neutral tone mapping.
- Authored glTF tangents, secondary UVs, and imported punctual lights.
- A native PBR Studio fixture and Mac capture mode using a Blender-exported
  antique camera.
- Screen-space contact occlusion and glTF clearcoat with independent factor,
  roughness, and normal maps. The studio accepts a local glTF or GLB subject.
- Authored glTF camera views for finite and infinite perspective and
  orthographic projections, with animated node poses and both Metal depth modes.
- glTF optical transmission, volume absorption, and index of refraction.
  Scene graphs can refract an opaque HDR backdrop with roughness filtering.
- Procedural lathes, beveled boxes, contour extrusions, swept tubes, and
  parametric surfaces with texture coordinates and tangents.
- Seeded leatherette, brushed metal, and wood finishes, plus custom PBR maps
  generated by a Swift UV sampler. A code-built rangefinder and studio fixture
  show the geometry, textures, glass, and HDR lighting together.
- A progressive Metal path tracer for static PBR scenes, with rectangular
  emitters, indirect light, world-space reflections, rough dielectric refraction,
  thin-lens focus, and guided HDR denoising. The Lab adds a product studio and
  a controlled lighting room.
- Typed PBR surface graphs that generate Metal functions for raster and path
  tracing, with inspectable source and procedural math nodes.
- Per-dispatch path tracing light rigs, aimed area emitters, and a native
  studio panel for light power, size, angle, tint, and exposure.
- A scalar transmission mask for generated surface graphs, and a procedural
  iceberg Lab scene with frosted ice, refractive blue fractures, and open water.
- Typed shader graphs with vector types, texture sampling, uniforms that edit
  without recompiling, caller-written Metal functions, a Swift builder, and
  diagnostics that quote the generated line. Surface graphs set normals,
  occlusion, and vertex offsets in raster PBR and the path tracer.
- Post-process graphs and bypassable chains, and compute graphs that generate
  textures, PBR maps, and buffer updates.
- Spot, point, and rectangle-light shadows; rectangular area lights; path-traced
  HDR environment maps and analytic lights; and `LightRig` for both renderers.
- Animation mixing with cross-fades and additive layers, glTF morph target
  import, orbit and fly camera controllers, BVH picking, frustum tests, and
  glTF and GLB export.
- Shader, Light, and Animation Lab scenes with generated inspectors, captures,
  and offscreen benchmarks, plus a Three.js parity checklist.
- GPU particles simulated by compute graphs, and an Effects Lab scene.
- glTF texture transforms and the emissive strength, specular, sheen,
  anisotropy, iridescence, dispersion, unlit, and variants material extensions
  in raster PBR and the path tracer.
- Quantized and meshopt-compressed glTF meshes, KTX2 block-compressed textures,
  WebP and AVIF images, and glTF GPU instancing data.
- GPU instancing, per-draw GPU morph targets, frustum culling with draw
  statistics, and distance LOD.
- Path tracer transform and geometry updates on a two-level BVH, skinned meshes,
  scrambled Sobol sampling, a firefly clamp, and GPU pass timing.
- glTF export of texture transforms, the material extensions, material
  variants, KTX2, WebP, and AVIF images, and GPU instances, with warnings for
  what glTF cannot store.
- Path tracing of imported glTF materials: `TextureSampling` describes a
  binding's sampler so the path tracer honors per-axis clamp, repeat, and
  mirrored addressing and nearest or linear magnification. Blended surfaces
  trace as unbiased stochastic opacity, including in shadow rays, and
  material-alpha surfaces write their alpha as raster does. Raster draws and
  scene graph export read the same description.
  [Evidence](docs/evidence/materials-and-assets.md)

### Fixed

- Path-traced normal maps on back faces now tilt the same way as in raster.
- Path-traced graph textures with clamp or mirror addressing no longer blend in
  the opposite edge at coarse mip levels.
- Graph textures in 32-bit float formats are rejected for linear filtering on
  GPUs that cannot filter them.
